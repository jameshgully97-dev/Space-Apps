    About the data

    Conflict events come from the GDELT 2.0 Event Database, which machine-codes world news every 15 minutes. Events are plotted where the article places the action; country-level geocodes are dropped. Coding is automated and can be wrong, duplicated or refer to past events — open the source articles before relying on any single marker.

    Soldiers, tanks, artillery, jets, drones and ships shown around an event are symbolic animations of the reported event type. No public source publishes live positions of individual troops or vehicles, and this app does not attempt to.

    Aircraft are real, live ADS-B positions from community receivers (adsb.lol, airplanes.live), drawn with a silhouette matching the reported ICAO aircraft type. Many military aircraft fly without transponders over active war zones, so coverage there is partial.

    Imagery: Esri World Imagery, NASA Black Marble night lights, Natural Earth II, and optionally Google Photorealistic 3D Tiles.
